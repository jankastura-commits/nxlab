Privacy Policy

Effective from 16 November 2026

1. Who controls your data

The controller of personal data is NOVIDA42 s.r.o., with its registered office at Rostoklaty 191, 281 71 Rostoklaty, Czech Republic, Company ID (IČO) 19593937, registered in the Commercial Register kept by the Municipal Court in Prague, Section C, File 388935 ("we"). For privacy matters, write to podpora@nxlab.ai or to our registered office. We have not appointed a data protection officer because the law does not require us to.

2. What this policy covers

This policy describes how we handle the data of people who visit nxlab.ai or write to us through it. Data processing in the course of services we provide to clients is governed by the contract with the client, including a data processing agreement.

3. What data we process and why

Contact form

  • Data: name, email, company name (optional) and the text of your message.
  • Purpose: replying to your message and discussing possible cooperation.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR), otherwise our legitimate interest in answering incoming messages (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: 2 years from our last communication. If we enter into a contract, for its duration and then for the statutory periods.

Application form

  • Data: name, contact (email, LinkedIn or GitHub), short introduction, example of your work, skills and availability.
  • Purpose: selecting people to work with.
  • Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).
  • Retention: until the selection is closed, at most 6 months from submission. Longer only with your consent.

Traffic measurement

  • Data: device and browser information, approximate location derived from your IP address, pages visited and an identifier stored in cookies.
  • Purpose: understanding how people use the site so that we can improve it.
  • Legal basis: your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). Measurement does not start without it.
  • Retention: at most 14 months.

Technical operation of the site

  • Data: IP address, time of access, requested page and browser information in hosting logs.
  • Purpose: displaying the site and keeping it secure.
  • Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working and secure website (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Retention: for as long as the hosting provider keeps the logs.

4. Cookies and third-party content

  • Without consent we store in your browser only a record of your cookie choice (local storage, key nx-consent).
  • With consent Google Analytics stores the cookies _ga and _ga_*, valid for up to 2 years.
  • You can change your choice at any time: open cookie settings.

The site loads fonts from Google Fonts, a style library from cdn.tailwindcss.com and an illustrative photo from Unsplash. When a page loads, your IP address and browser information are therefore technically transmitted to the operators of these services.

5. Who we share data with

  • Netlify, Inc. (USA): website hosting and receipt of form submissions.
  • Google Ireland Limited: Google Analytics (only with your consent) and Google Fonts.
  • Providers of the email and office services we use to handle messages.
  • Accounting and legal advisers where needed to handle a matter, and public authorities where the law requires it.

We do not sell data and do not pass it to anyone for their own marketing.

6. Transfers outside the European Union

Some of these providers are based in the United States or process data there. Transfers rely on the European Commission's adequacy decision (EU-US Data Privacy Framework) for providers certified under it, and otherwise on standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission.

7. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • obtain confirmation of whether we process your data, and a copy of it,
  • have inaccurate data corrected,
  • ask for erasure of your data or restriction of its processing,
  • object to processing that we base on legitimate interest,
  • receive the data you gave us in a machine-readable format,
  • withdraw your consent to traffic measurement at any time, without affecting processing before the withdrawal,
  • lodge a complaint with the Czech Office for Personal Data Protection, Pplk. Sochora 27, 170 00 Praha 7, www.uoou.gov.cz.

We will respond to a request without undue delay and within one month at the latest.

8. Automated decision-making

We do not use data from the website for automated decision-making or profiling that would have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

9. Do you have to give us your data?

No, providing data is voluntary. Without a name and email, however, we cannot reply to a message sent through the form.

10. Security

The site runs over an encrypted connection and only the people handling incoming messages have access to them. Please do not send confidential materials or sensitive data through the forms. In such cases we will first agree on a secure way to share them.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example when we add a new feature to the site. The current version is always on this page, with the date from which it applies. If the Czech and English versions differ, the Czech version prevails.
